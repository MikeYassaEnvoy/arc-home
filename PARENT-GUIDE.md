# Parent guide — The Amazing Race: Home Edition

Five legs around the house, playable in one rainy Saturday or spread across
a week of evenings. Full detail for every leg: each step, every challenge
with its checklist, quiz answers (your cheat sheet — he never sees this),
timers, photo prompts and points. Legs unlock in order (finish a leg's pit
stop to unlock the next). Season 2 story: his four rivals from the Québec
trip are BACK for a rematch, and the Rock Hoppers want revenge for the
photo finish.

## The hidden parent menu

Tap the little **version number ("v0.1.0")** in the corner of Race HQ
**7 times**. From there you can:

- **Jump to a leg** (unlocks it and everything before it) — use this if the
  race gets split across days or played out of order.
- **Mark steps complete** — skip anything that doesn't work in your house.
- **Award a badge** manually.
- **Content check** — confirms all the day's content loaded correctly.
- **🏁 Arm the finish line** (long-press) — the finale mat is LOCKED until
  you flip this. Do it quietly before he heads for the driveway.
- **🏆 Replay the finale** — re-runs the whole finish sequence (photo-finish,
  trophy, recap, photo reel) any time. Replay-safe: no duplicate history or
  points. Perfect for grandparents.
- **Full reset** (long-press) — wipes everything. This is how you start a
  brand-new season later.

Kids can also long-press "Skip this stop" on any step — it's honor-system
everywhere; nothing ever requires a photo or a parent password.

## Scoring, badges, rivals (so you can play along)

- Every challenge lists its points; mini-games add up to 10 bonus points per
  session (only the first 8 sessions per leg count, so replaying is harmless).
- Placements are **scripted** — challenge scores never change them. The
  ceremonies explain placements as "time on the course"; points are his
  season score. The arc: 2nd → 4th (the low point) → 1st (comeback!) →
  2nd (heartbreaker) → CHAMPION in a photo finish.
- Badges: one per leg + specials (⭐ First Win, 🇫🇷 French Speaker at the
  Café Français, 📸 Photographer after 10 photos, 🙈 Brave Bite for the
  blindfold taste test — that one is auto-awarded by the challenge).

## Leg 0 — The Rematch Begins (opening ceremony, ~5 minutes)

**Race morning — 📍 Route Marker: Front Door Starting Line**
- Clue says: "Stand by the front door with your team. Answer three warm-up questions. Then step on the mat to meet your rivals."
- **Champion's Warm-Up** (trivia · 5 pts) — Three quick questions before the race. A champion remembers everything. Guessing is still allowed!
  - Q: Which team did you beat in last summer's photo finish? → **The Rock Hoppers**
  - Q: Where is today's whole race happening? → **Right here at home**
  - Q: What do you rip open to get your next clue? → **An envelope**

**Race morning — 🏁 Pit Stop: Home Starting Line**
- **Ceremony — "Meet the Teams: The Rematch":** Jon welcomes the defending champions and re-introduces all four rivals. No placements. Badge: 🎒 Back in the Race.

**Prep:** Just hand over the iPad by the front door. Three warm-up questions, then the ceremony re-introduces all four rival teams with rematch storylines. Warm-up points don't count toward the season.

## Leg 1 — Kitchen Confidential (Front Door → Kitchen)

**Leg 1 · Stop 1 — 📍 Route Marker: Kitchen Counter HQ**
- Clue says: "Race to the kitchen counter. A parent will pick three tiny mystery bites. Close your eyes and taste each one!"
- **Mystery Bite** (taste · 15 pts) — Close your eyes tight — no peeking. A parent hands you three tiny bites. Guess each one, then rate your favourite.
  - List: Bite one — I made my guess · Bite two — I made my guess · Bite three — I made my guess · At least one guess was right!
  - 📸 Blindfold taste-test face!

**Leg 1 · Stop 2 — 📍 Route Marker: The Great Kitchen Census**
- Clue says: "Stay in the kitchen for a counting mission. Count every spoon in the drawer. Then count everything stuck to the fridge."
- **Spoon Census** (count · 8 pts) — Open the cutlery drawer. Count every single spoon — big and small. Type your number in the box.
- **Fridge Door Count** (count · 7 pts) — Look at the outside of the fridge. Count every magnet, photo and note. Nothing on the door escapes you.

**Leg 1 · Stop 3 — 🚧 Roadblock: The Dinner Table**
- Clue says: "Who's ready to be a waiter? Set the table for your whole family. Beat three minutes on the stopwatch."
- **Table Setting Sprint** (physical · 15 pts · stopwatch target 3:00) — Start the stopwatch, then set the table. Every person needs a plate, a fork and a cup. Straighten everything before you stop the clock!
  - 📸 Fanciest waiter pose!

**Leg 1 · Stop 4 — 🚗 Drive: Snack Break Couch**
- Clue says: "Take a couch break — you earned it. Play the Kitchen Trivia deck. Then race to the kitchen table mat!"
- **Kitchen Trivia Deck** (minigame · 10 pts) — Ten tasty questions about food and kitchens. One big question at a time. Streaks light a little fire!
  - Mini-game: `trivia` (deck: leg-1, count: 10)

**Leg 1 · Pit Stop — 🏁 Pit Stop: The Kitchen Table Mat**
- **Mat: 2nd of 5** — Team Maple edges it by forty seconds, of course. Non-elimination ("everyone races on!"). Badge: 🍳 Kitchen Captain.

**Prep:** Before the leg, secretly pick **three mystery foods** for the blindfold taste test — one easy (banana), one trickier (a bit of cheese), one silly-but-safe (a pickle). Small bites on a spoon work best. The Brave Bite badge is auto-awarded when he completes it. For the table-setting roadblock, just point at the cupboards and let him go.

## Leg 2 — Backyard Expedition (Kitchen → Backyard)

*(Rain plan: every challenge works indoors — scavenger out a window, window count from inside, obstacle laps around the living room. Nothing needs to be skipped.)*

**Leg 2 · Stop 1 — 📍 Route Marker: Explorer Training Grounds**
- Clue says: "Head out to the backyard. This is Explorer Training: Level Two. Find all six things on the list."
- **Explorer Training: Level Two** (scavenger · 15 pts) — Last summer's training was the easy version. Find all six things. Look low, look high, look under.
  - List: Two different kinds of bugs · A stick shaped like the letter Y · Something a bird could eat · The softest thing in the yard · A rock with more than one colour · Something that was not here last week
  - 📸 Explorer at work!

**Leg 2 · Stop 2 — ⚡ Speed Bump: The Middle of the Yard**
- Clue says: "Stand in the middle of the yard. Turn in a slow circle. Count every window you can see on your house."
- **Window Watch** (count · 8 pts) — Turn slowly and scan your whole house. Count every window you can see. Type your number in the box.

**Leg 2 · Stop 3 — 🚧 Roadblock: Backyard Grand Prix**
- Clue says: "Who's got the fastest feet? Run the backyard obstacle circuit. Beat two minutes on the stopwatch!"
- **Backyard Obstacle Grand Prix** (physical · 15 pts · stopwatch target 2:00) — Start the clock and do every move on the list. Go as fast as you safely can. Finish before two minutes run out!
  - List: Run two laps of the yard · Do ten jumping jacks · Touch the fence or the farthest wall · Spin around three times · Strike a superhero landing pose
  - 📸 Superhero landing!

**Leg 2 · Stop 4 — 🚗 Drive: Porch Pit Row**
- Clue says: "Sit on the porch or a step. Play Backyard Bingo with your eyes on the yard. Then take the nature quiz."
- **Backyard Bingo** (minigame · 10 pts) — Look around the yard for each square. Tap it when you spot it. Four in a row is a bingo!
  - Mini-game: `road-bingo` (card: backyard)
- **Nature Trivia Deck** (minigame · 10 pts) — Ten questions about bugs, birds and backyards. Guess before you tap. Every answer has a fun fact.
  - Mini-game: `trivia` (deck: leg-2, count: 10)

**Leg 2 · Pit Stop — 🏁 Pit Stop: The Backyard Mat**
- **Mat: 4th of 5** — the rough night; **Prairie Thunder eliminated** (they got lost between two rooms of one house). Badge: 🌿 Backyard Explorer.

**Prep:** Zero setup. The scavenger list is deliberately open-ended ("something that was not here last week" accepts any honest answer). The scripted 4th place lands at this mat — it's his low point of the season, so play the comeback tease warmly.

## Leg 3 — Secrets of the Second Floor (Backyard → Upstairs)

*(No second floor? Rename it in your head — any bedroom + hallway works; the app never checks.)*

**Leg 3 · Stop 1 — 📍 Route Marker: Your Bedroom**
- Clue says: "Race up to your own bedroom. Become a detective in your own room. Find all five things on the list."
- **Bedroom Detective** (scavenger · 15 pts) — Your own room is the crime scene. Find each thing on the list. Tap to check it off.
  - List: Something from the Québec trip · The oldest toy you own · Something you made yourself · The softest thing in the room · Something you have not played with in ages

**Leg 3 · Stop 2 — 🔀 DETOUR — choose ONE branch: The Upstairs Hallway**
- Clue says: "This is a Detour: pick Build or Books. Build a rolling machine, or hunt six special books. Choose the one you will be best at!"
- **Branch A — "Build":** Ten minutes, one rolling machine. Builders, start your engines.
  - **The Ten-Minute Machine** (physical · 20 pts · stopwatch target 10:00) — Build something with wheels that really rolls. Use Lego or any blocks you own. Ten minutes on the clock!
    - List: It has wheels · It rolls when you push it · It has an awesome name
    - 📸 Show off your machine!
- **Branch B — "Books":** Six special books are hiding on your own shelf.
  - **Home Book Hunt** (scavenger · 20 pts) — Search every bookshelf in the house. Find each kind of book. Put every book back where it was.
    - List: A book with an animal on the cover · A book with a number in the title · A book with your first initial in the title · A book you have read more than once · The dustiest book you can find · A book taller than your hand

**Leg 3 · Stop 3 — 🚧 Roadblock: The Laundry Pile**
- Clue says: "Who's the Sock Ninja? A parent dumps out the sock pile. Match eight pairs before three minutes run out!"
- **Sock Match Showdown** (physical · 20 pts · stopwatch target 3:00) — Start the stopwatch, then dig in. Match eight pairs and fold each pair together. Beat three minutes to earn your ninja belt!
  - 📸 Sock pyramid victory pose!

**Leg 3 · Stop 4 — ⚡ Speed Bump: Mission: Fresh Bed**
- Clue says: "Quick bonus mission! Make your bed like a fancy hotel bed. Smooth blanket, straight pillows, no lumps."
- **Five-Star Bed** (physical · 5 pts) — Pull the blanket smooth and flat. Line the pillows up straight. Add one stuffed animal as the fancy touch.

**Leg 3 · Pit Stop — 🏁 Pit Stop: Top of the Stairs Mat**
- **Mat: 1st — FIRST WIN**, confetti; **The Tide Riders eliminated** (found asleep in a blanket fort). Badges: 🧦 Sock Ninja + ⭐ First Win.

**Prep:** Dump **at least 8 matchable sock pairs** in a pile before the roadblock (mix in a few odd socks for difficulty). The detour needs either a Lego/blocks bin (Build) or a bookshelf (Books) — both branches score the same, let him choose. This is the comeback leg: first place, first win of the season.

## Leg 4 — The Couch 500 (Upstairs → Living Room)

*(The screen-time leg: two arcade heats bracket real-world stops, same rhythm as the trip's driving days.)*

**Leg 4 · Stop 1 — 🚗 Drive: Couch Grand Prix — Heat One**
- Clue says: "Take the best seat on the couch. Heat One has two games: the rapids and the maze. Beat them both!"
- **Canoe the Rapids** (minigame · 10 pts) — Drag your finger to steer the canoe. Dodge every rock. Survive sixty seconds!
  - Mini-game: `canoe-rapids`
- **Maze Escape** (minigame · 10 pts) — Drag your racer to the red exit gate. No cutting through walls. Champions do not give up!
  - Mini-game: `maze-escape` (level: 2)
- **Animal Trivia Encore** (minigame · 10 pts) — The famous animal deck from the trip is back. Twelve questions this time. Can you still beat it?
  - Mini-game: `trivia` (deck: biodome, count: 12)

**Leg 4 · Stop 2 — ⚡ Speed Bump: Pit Crew Snack Service**
- Clue says: "Speed bump! Make a small snack or drink for every person home. Deliver it all in three minutes."
- **Pit Crew Snack Run** (physical · 10 pts · stopwatch target 3:00) — Start the stopwatch in the kitchen. Bring every team member a snack or a drink. Careful hands — no spills!
  - 📸 Snack delivery in action!

**Leg 4 · Stop 3 — 📍 Route Marker: Café Français**
- Clue says: "A parent is now a French café waiter. Order your snack in French. Then taste it and judge it like a pro."
- **Order in French** (speak-french · 5 pts) — Practise the phrase on the card twice. Then say it to the café waiter. Do not forget to say merci!
  - Phrase: *Un biscuit, s'il vous plaît.* ("uhn bee-SKWEE, seel voo PLEH") = One cookie, please.
- **Snack Judge** (taste · 5 pts) — Taste your café order slowly. Think about the crunch and the flavour. Give it your maple leaf rating.
  - 📸 Fancy café customer face!

**Leg 4 · Stop 4 — 🚗 Drive: Couch Grand Prix — Heat Two**
- Clue says: "Heat Two, back on the couch! Play Bagel Catch, then House Bingo. Then race to the living room mat."
- **Bagel Catch Championship** (minigame · 10 pts) — Slide the basket to catch falling bagels. Dodge the pigeons. Beat your best score from the trip!
  - Mini-game: `bagel-catch`
- **House Bingo** (minigame · 10 pts) — A brand-new card of things inside the house. Spot each thing and tap its square. Four in a row wins!
  - Mini-game: `road-bingo` (card: house)

**Leg 4 · Pit Stop — 🏁 Pit Stop: The Living Room Mat**
- **Mat: 2nd of 3** — the Rock Hoppers win the leg; **Team Maple eliminated** (last for the first time in two seasons). Badge: 🕹️ Arcade Ace.

**Prep:** You have two cameo roles: snack recipient (Pit Crew Snack Run) and **French café waiter** — he orders "Un biscuit, s'il vous plaît" and you hand over a cookie with maximum Parisian dignity. Saying it aloud auto-awards 🇫🇷 French Speaker. The mat sets up the finale: Maple is finally out, and it's the Rock Hoppers rematch.

## Leg 5 — THE FINALE: Race for the House Cup (Living Room → Driveway)

**Finale · Stop 1 — 📍 Route Marker: The Memory Lap**
- Clue says: "Run one lap through the whole race. Collect one thing from each leg's room. Bring them all back to the living room."
- **The Memory Lap** (scavenger · 15 pts) — Visit every room you raced in today. Collect each item on the list. Line them up like trophies when you are done.
  - List: A spoon from the kitchen leg · A leaf or rock from the backyard · One folded sock pair from upstairs · Something from the Café Français · The comfiest cushion from the Couch 500
  - 📸 Your trophy collection, all lined up!

**Finale · Stop 2 — 🚧 Roadblock: The Recap Round**
- Clue says: "Who remembers it all? Answer five questions about TODAY'S race. You lived every answer!"
- **The Recap Round** (trivia · 15 pts) — Five questions about your race today. No hints, no help. Prove you remember it all!
  - Q: Where did you taste the three mystery bites? → **The kitchen**
  - Q: How many sock pairs did the Sock Ninja have to match? → **Eight**
  - Q: What did you order at Café Français? → **Un biscuit**
  - Q: Which team was eliminated FIRST this season? → **Prairie Thunder**
  - Q: Which team are you racing in this finale? → **The Rock Hoppers**

**Finale · Stop 3 — ⚡ Speed Bump: Double Lap Dash: The Rematch**
- Clue says: "The very first challenge you ever did — again! Run two laps around the house or yard. Beat sixty seconds one more time."
- **Double Lap Dash: The Rematch** (physical · 10 pts · stopwatch target 1:00) — Tap start, then run two full laps. You have trained a whole year for this. Beat sixty seconds!
  - 📸 Flying finish!

**Finale · Stop 4 — 🚗 Drive: The Final Puzzle**
- Clue says: "Almost there! Play one last game about today's race. Match every photo to its stop, in order."
- **Photo Finish** (minigame · 25 pts) — Look at the photos from today's race. Match each one to its stop. Put them in the right order!
  - Mini-game: `photo-finish`

**Finale · The Finish — 🏁 Pit Stop: THE DRIVEWAY — FINISH LINE**
- **FINISH LINE: photo-finish WIN — BACK-TO-BACK CHAMPIONS!** Trophy, confetti storm, full season recap. Badge: 🏆 Race Champion.

**Prep:** **Photo Finish uses the photos he took today** — nudge him to use the optional camera button during earlier legs so the final puzzle has material (4+ photos is plenty). Tell him **press and hold any photo to see it big**. **The finish line is LOCKED** until you arm it in the parent menu — do the long-press quietly while he plays Photo Finish, then let him run out the front door to a real mat/towel on the driveway before opening the final pit stop. The finale is ~2 minutes of pure payoff. Don't skip the recap screen; every photo from the day is in it.
